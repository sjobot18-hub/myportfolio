About Me

I work at the point where customer experience, data quality and day-to-day operations meet. Most of my work involves handling real customer conversations, checking that information is correct before it goes out, and keeping the systems behind an online business running without noise.

On the data side, I annotate, label and evaluate content against written guidelines. Reading them closely, applying them consistently, and flagging the edge cases instead of guessing. The same habit carries into e-commerce work: accurate listings, clean product copy, orders that get processed and tracked properly, and issues resolved before a customer has to follow up twice.

I write clearly, document what I do, and work well without supervision which matters in a remote team across time zones. I'm comfortable learning a new tool or workflow quickly and adapting when priorities shift.

Alongside this, I shoot and edit product and lifestyle content on mobile. It's not a separate career: knowing how a product is presented visually makes me better at writing about it, listing it, and supporting the customers who buy it.
